# Habit Context: LeetCode

## Why
To build consistent problem-solving reps for interviews without turning every session into a willpower contest. The goal is steady exposure, not occasional heroics.

## Rules of Engagement
- **Frequency:** Daily.
- **Primary Win Condition:** Solve one new LeetCode question.
- **Backup Win Condition:** If a new problem is too heavy for the day, either solve one LeetCode easy or review a past medium thoroughly enough to explain the approach back to yourself.
- **Definition of Failure:** ❌ only if none of the above happens.
- **Note Format:** Log the difficulty and the concrete result. Example: "Solved new medium in 60m" or "Reviewed past medium, re-derived two-pointer approach."

## The Trigger
- **Primary:** Start after the first serious work block begins.
- **Implementation Intention:** "Once I sit down for my first focused block, I will do one LeetCode rep before drifting into random input."

## Backup Protocols
- **If** energy is low, **Then** do an easy.
- **If** attention is fragmented, **Then** review a past medium and write down the key pattern.
- **If** a problem gets stuck, **Then** timebox the attempt, extract the lesson, and decide whether it still counts as a review instead of pretending it was a full solve.

## Coaching Notes
- Protect consistency first. Difficulty is secondary to repetition.
- Watch for fake wins where the problem is "done" but the method is still fuzzy.
- A clean review counts when it improves recall and pattern recognition.
