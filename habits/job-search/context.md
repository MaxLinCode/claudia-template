# Habit Context: Job Search

## Why
To rebuild job-search momentum through daily reps instead of waiting for motivation. The habit is designed around evidence-backed behavior principles: reduce activation energy, use a clear implementation intention, keep the minimum viable action small, and separate consistency from intensity.

## Rules of Engagement
- **Frequency:** Daily.
- **Minimum Viable Threshold:** Complete one focused 15-minute job-search block.
  - Acceptable outcomes for a ✅:
    - Submit one job application.
    - Send one high-quality networking message tied to an open role.
    - Tailor one resume or cover letter for a specific role.
    - Build and save a shortlist of three realistic roles to apply to next.
- **Definition of Failure:** ❌ only if no 15-minute block happens.
- **Note Format:** Log the concrete output, not just effort. Example: "15m, tailored resume for Stripe SWE role" or "Applied to 1 role at Figma."

## The Trigger
- **Primary:** Start the block immediately after opening your laptop for the first serious work session of the day.
- **Implementation Intention:** "After I open my laptop and sit down to work, I will spend 15 minutes on job search before drifting into lower-leverage tasks."
- **Environment Design:** Keep resume, master cover letter, job tracker, and bookmarked job boards open or one click away.

## Backup Protocols
- **If** energy is low, **Then** do a lighter 15-minute block by saving three roles or drafting bullet points for one application.
- **If** avoidance is high, **Then** set a 5-minute starter timer and only commit to opening one role plus editing one line. If momentum builds, continue. If not, log the day based on whether the full 15-minute block happened.
- **If** there are no good roles today, **Then** use the block for networking outreach or resume improvement tied to a target company.

## Coaching Notes
- Prioritize streak protection over heroic volume.
- Raise the bar only after two stable weeks of consistency.
- Look for friction patterns in notes: fear of rejection, unclear targets, resume friction, or energy timing.
