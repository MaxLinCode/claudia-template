Don't accelerate into the next shot; take it nice and smooth. Turn your toes out for the recovery shot so that you can control your shot and balance. Stay lower with a wide stance to get better stability and push off. Take big steps like a compass. Narrow stance blocks your flow. For the drop shot, take it nice and smooth, using the natural rotation of the arm, wrist, and fingers. Make sure to be flexible in the hips and twist.
