# Habit Context: Vitamins

## Why
To stay consistent with the daily vitamins and supplements that support baseline health, recovery, and training. The goal is to make this automatic instead of random.

## Rules of Engagement
- **Frequency:** Daily.
- **Primary Win Condition:** Take the regular supplement stack for the day.
- **Fallback Win Condition:** If the full stack does not happen, take one daily multivitamin.
- **Definition of Failure:** ❌ only if neither the regular stack nor the fallback multivitamin happens.
- **Note Format:** Log what was actually taken. Example: "Multivitamin only" or "Multivitamin, magnesium glycinate, fish oil, vitamin D."

## The Trigger
- **Primary:** Take vitamins with the first meal of the day.
- **Implementation Intention:** "When I eat my first real meal, I take my vitamins before I leave the kitchen."

## Backup Protocols
- **If** the full stack feels annoying, **Then** take the multivitamin and count the fallback win.
- **If** you forget earlier, **Then** take them with dinner instead of writing off the day.
- **If** you are running low on something, **Then** take what is available and note what is missing.

## Coaching Notes
- Protect the baseline. The fallback exists to keep the habit alive.
- The real win is making this automatic, not perfect.
- Watch whether creatine stays in the "future me" category or actually becomes part of the stack.
